Who Can Enter

Our campus short film competition is created specifically for student filmmakers. If you are currently studying at a recognised college or university, the Campus Category gives you an opportunity to showcase your filmmaking talent on a larger platform.

The Campus Track is reserved for currently enrolled students and requires proof of current student status.

Not a student? Enter the General Category, open to eligible filmmakers worldwide.

Proof of Student Status

Campus Track submissions require one valid proof of current student status — a college/university ID card, an official institution verification document, or an official letter on the institution's letterhead — uploaded as a PDF or JPG (max 5 MB) in the submission form.

Film Requirements

  • Runtime

    Films must not exceed 30 minutes, including opening and closing credits.

  • Production Dates

    Films must have been produced between January 1, 2025 and December 31, 2026. Works-in-progress are not accepted.

  • Subtitles

    Films in any language other than Malayalam must include English subtitles. Malayalam-language films do not require English subtitles.

  • Rights

    You must own, or have all necessary rights and licences for, all content in the film, including footage, music and any third-party material.
