Who Can Enter

For independent filmmakers, production teams and creators from anywhere in the world. Whether you are an emerging filmmaker or an experienced creator, submit your original short film and compete for recognition across the official award categories.

The General Track is open to filmmakers of all nationalities. Participants must be at least 18 years of age, or have the consent of a parent or guardian if under 18.

Currently a student? The Campus Category is dedicated to student filmmakers.

Film Requirements

  • Runtime

    Short films must have a maximum runtime of 40 minutes, including opening and closing credits. There is no fixed minimum runtime.

  • Production Dates

    Films must have been produced between January 1, 2025 and December 31, 2026. Works-in-progress are not accepted.

  • Subtitles

    Films in any language other than Malayalam must include English subtitles. Malayalam-language films do not require English subtitles.

  • Rights

    You must own, or have all necessary rights and licences for, all content in the film, including footage, music and any third-party material.

Key Dates & Entry Fee

  • Entry Deadline

    31 December 2026

  • Entry Fee

    ₹1,000 per film, paid securely online via Cashfree. Each film is a separate entry.

  • Awards Ceremony

    12 February 2027 — Grand Function and winners' announcement at JAIN (Deemed-to-be University), Kochi Campus, Kerala.
