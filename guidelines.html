Submission Rules

  1. 01

    1. Runtime Duration

    All project submissions must keep total run times strictly at or underneath a maximum runtime limit of 30 minutes including credits.

  2. 02

    2. Eligibility & Timeline

    Films must have been produced between January 1, 2025 and December 31, 2026. Works-in-progress are not accepted.

  3. 03

    3. Original Content & Copyright

    Applicants must hold all clear intellectual rights, licenses, and permits regarding both music tracks and imagery materials utilized inside the submission.

  4. 04

    4. Campus Track Requirements

    Campus Track submissions require one valid proof of current student status — a college/university ID card, an official institution verification document, or an official letter on the institution's letterhead — uploaded as a PDF or JPG (max 5 MB) in the submission form.

  5. 05

    5. Review Panel Verdicts

    Decisions and evaluations handed down by the official judging board remain absolute, terminal, and unalterable.

Festival Guidelines

  1. 01

    1. Screening Conduct

    Attendees and finalists are expected to remain seated for the full duration of each screening block. Mobile devices should be silenced inside the auditorium.

  2. 02

    2. Photography & Recording

    Personal photography is welcome in lobby and lounge areas. Recording of screened films from inside the auditorium is strictly prohibited to protect filmmakers' copyright.

  3. 03

    3. Accreditation & Entry

    A valid e-pass or entry confirmation (sent after registration) must be presented at the venue check-in desk for admission to the festival and awards night.

  4. 04

    4. Code of Conduct

    Angle Frames maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward harassment, discrimination, or disruptive behavior of any kind toward fellow attendees, filmmakers, or staff.

  5. 05

    5. Schedule Changes

    Screening order and program timings are subject to last-minute adjustment. Please check official festival updates and on-site signage for the most current schedule.
