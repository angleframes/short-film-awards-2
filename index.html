About Angle Frames[cite: 1]

Angle Frames stands as a premier creative collective and platform committed to fueling the fires of independent filmmaking. Founded with a vision to build bridges for raw, unadulterated storytelling, Angle Frames discovers, highlights, and honors ground-breaking visual concepts. By organizing top-tier competitive arenas, masterclasses, and project distributions, we give voice to young digital visionaries across the region.[cite: 1]

About Jain University[cite: 1]

Ranked consistently among India’s top educational institutions, JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) acts as a powerful catalyst for human development and progressive learning, specifically within its creative and media programs. Through its collaboration on the Sharankrishna Short Film Awards, Jain University reinforces its institutional drive toward nurturing industry-ready artistic talents.[cite: 1]

Who is Sharankrishna?[cite: 1]

Sharankrishna was a vibrant creative spirit whose life was anchored deeply in the world of independent filmmaking and visual storytelling. Cinema was an absolute passion, a canvas for honest thoughts, and an arena for boundless raw expression.[cite: 1]

The Story Behind Our Logo[cite: 1]

The core visual structure integrates a gold-toned camera aperture frame enclosing geometric perspectives. The closing aperture signifies pinning down a transient slice of time into a permanent timeline.[cite: 1]

Rules & Guidelines[cite: 1]

1. Runtime Duration[cite: 1]

All project submissions must keep total run times strictly at or underneath a maximum runtime limit of 30 minutes including credits.[cite: 1]

2. Eligibility & Timeline[cite: 1]

Films must have been completed on or after January 1, 2024. Works-in-progress are not accepted.[cite: 1]

3. Original Content & Copyright[cite: 1]

Applicants must hold all clear intellectual rights, licenses, and permits regarding both music tracks and imagery materials utilized inside the submission.[cite: 1]

4. Campus Track Requirements[cite: 1]
