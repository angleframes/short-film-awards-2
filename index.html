About Angle Frames

Angle Frames stands as a premier creative collective and platform committed to fueling the fires of independent filmmaking. Founded with a vision to build bridges for raw, unadulterated storytelling, Angle Frames discovers, highlights, and honors ground-breaking visual concepts. By organizing top-tier competitive arenas, masterclasses, and project distributions, we give voice to young digital visionaries across the region.

About Jain University

Ranked consistently among India's top educational institutions, JAIN (Deemed-to-be University) acts as a powerful catalyst for human development and progressive learning. The Kochi Campus emphasizes creative exploration and technical mastery, specifically within its creative and media programs. Through its collaboration on the Sharankrishna Short Film Awards, Jain University reinforces its institutional drive toward nurturing industry-ready artistic talents.

Who is Sharankrishna?

Sharankrishna was a vibrant creative spirit whose life was anchored deeply in the world of independent filmmaking and visual storytelling. Cinema was an absolute passion, a canvas for honest thoughts, and an arena for boundless raw expression.

The Story Behind Our Logo

The core visual structure integrates a gold-toned camera aperture frame enclosing geometric perspectives. The closing aperture signifies pinning down a transient slice of time into a permanent timeline.

Rules & Guidelines

1. Runtime Duration

All project submissions must keep total run times strictly at or underneath a maximum runtime limit of 30 minutes including credits.

2. Eligibility & Timeline

Films must have been completed on or after January 1, 2024. Works-in-progress are not accepted.

3. Original Content & Copyright

Applicants must hold all clear intellectual rights, licenses, and permits regarding both music tracks and imagery materials utilized inside the submission.

4. Campus Track Requirements

Submissions for the student division require proof of active institutional enrollment (Student ID Card Scan upload validation).

5. Review Panel Verdicts

Decisions and evaluations handed down by the official judging board remain absolute, terminal, and unalterable.

How To Submit

1. Your Details

Start the entry wizard with your name, email, phone number, and city so we can reach you about your submission.

2. Film Details

Add your film's title, category, runtime, and a working link to the film hosted on YouTube or a cloud storage service.

3. Crew & Cast Credits

List your director, producer, writer, cinematographer, editor, music director, and lead cast members so the credits are accurately recorded.

4. Entry Fee Payment

Pay the entry fee via the UPI QR code shown in the portal, then upload your payment screenshot or PDF along with the transaction reference ID.

5. Review & Confirm

Double-check your entry summary on the final step, then submit. You'll see an on-screen confirmation once your entry has been received.

Information We Collect

When you register a film entry, we collect details you provide such as your name, email, phone number, city, film and crew information, and payment reference details.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to process your submission, verify entry payments, communicate updates about the festival, and coordinate the judging process.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Limited details may be shared internally with the jury panel and organizing team strictly for evaluation purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by reaching out through our contact helpdesk.
