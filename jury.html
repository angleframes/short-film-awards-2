2026 Edition Jury

Sri. M Mohanan

Writer and Director

M. Mohanan is an Indian film director, writer and former assistant director working in Malayalam cinema. He began his career assisting Sathyan Anthikad on 14 films before making his directorial debut with Katha Parayumpol (2007). His notable films include Manikyakkallu (2011), 916 (2012), Aravindante Athidhikal (2018) and Oru Jaathi Jathakam (2025). He is also a recipient of Kerala State Film Awards for Best Story and Best Popular Film. Wikipedia

Sri Maneesh Narayanan

Journalist Film critic and media enterpreneur Co Founder The Che And Cue Studio

Maneesh Narayanan is a prominent film critic, journalist, interviewer and media professional in Malayalam media. He is the Co-founder, Editor-in-Chief and CEO of The Cue and Cue Studio, and is widely known for his in-depth conversations on cinema and culture. His career includes senior editorial roles at IndiaVision and SouthLive, and he has also served on film juries, including a Kerala State Film Development Corporation selection panel.
