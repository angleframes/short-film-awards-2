Who Can Enter

For independent filmmakers, production teams and creators from anywhere in the world. Whether you are an emerging filmmaker or an experienced creator, submit your original short film and compete for recognition across the official award categories.

The General Track is open to filmmakers of all nationalities. Participants must be at least 18 years of age, or have the consent of a parent or guardian if under 18.

Currently a student? The Campus Category is dedicated to student filmmakers.
