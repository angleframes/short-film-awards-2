How To Submit

Follow these five simple steps to register your film for Sharankrishna Short Film Awards.

How To Submit Guidelines Updates Jury Panel FAQ Privacy Terms Refunds
  1. 01

    Step 1: Your Details

    Start the entry wizard with your name, email, phone number, and city so we can reach you about your submission.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Film Details

    Add your film's title, category, runtime, and a working link to the film hosted on YouTube or a cloud storage service.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Crew & Cast Credits

    List your director, producer, writer, cinematographer, editor, music director, and lead cast members so the credits are accurately recorded.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Entry Fee Payment

    Pay the ₹1,000 entry fee securely inside the portal via Cashfree (UPI, cards, net banking, or wallets). Your payment is verified automatically — no screenshots or reference codes needed.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Review & Confirm

    Double-check your entry summary on the final step, then submit. You'll see an on-screen confirmation once your entry has been received.
