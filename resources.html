Getting Started

How To Submit

Follow these five simple steps to register your film for Sharankrishna Short Film Awards.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Your Details

    Start the entry wizard with your name, email, phone number, and city so we can reach you about your submission.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Film Details

    Add your film's title, category, runtime, and a working link to the film hosted on YouTube or a cloud storage service.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Crew & Cast Credits

    List your director, producer, writer, cinematographer, editor, music director, and lead cast members so the credits are accurately recorded.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Entry Fee Payment

    Pay the ₹1,000 entry fee securely inside the portal via Cashfree (UPI, cards, net banking, or wallets). Your payment is verified automatically — no screenshots or reference codes needed.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Review & Confirm

    Double-check your entry summary on the final step, then submit. You'll see an on-screen confirmation once your entry has been received.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Answers to common questions about eligibility, submission, fees, subtitles, multiple entries and awards.

    Sharankrishna Short Film Awards is a short film competition honoring the legacy of Sharankrishna, open to filmmakers of all nationalities. The festival runs a General Track open to all filmmakers and a Campus Track for college students, hosted by Angle Frames in association with JAIN (Deemed-to-be University), Kochi. Read the story behind the awards.

    The General Track is open to filmmakers of all nationalities. Participants must be at least 18 years of age, or have the consent of a parent or guardian if under 18. The Campus Track is for students and requires one valid proof of current student status (a college/university ID card, an official institution verification document, or a letter on the institution's letterhead), uploaded as a PDF or JPG in the submission form. Full requirements are listed in the Rules & Guidelines.

    Entries are submitted online through the registration form on this website. You enter your details, your film's title, category, runtime and a working YouTube or Drive link, and your cast & crew credits, then review your entry and pay the entry fee securely online. A step-by-step walkthrough is in How To Submit, or you can start your submission now.

    Entries close on December 31, 2026. Filmmakers are encouraged to follow the steps in How To Submit well ahead of the deadline.

    The entry fee is ₹1,000 per submission, paid securely online via Cashfree (UPI, cards, net banking or wallets). The fee is non-refundable once payment is confirmed — see Refunds & Cancellations for details.

    Films must not exceed 30 minutes, including opening and closing credits. Films must have been produced between January 1, 2025 and December 31, 2026. See the full Rules & Guidelines.

    The General Track is open to any filmmaker. The Campus Track is reserved for currently enrolled students and requires proof of current student status. See the Rules & Guidelines for track-specific requirements.

    Winners across the award categories are honoured at the Grand Function and winners' announcement on February 12, 2027. See all award categories.

    Yes. Filmmakers can submit multiple short films. Each film must be submitted as a separate entry, and the ₹1,000 entry fee must be paid separately for each submission. See How To Submit for the submission steps.

    Yes, if your film is in any language other than Malayalam. Films in any language other than Malayalam must include English subtitles. This allows the jury to understand and evaluate the film properly. Malayalam-language films do not require English subtitles. See the Rules & Guidelines for the other submission requirements.

    Entries are evaluated by the festival's official jury panel. Jury members will be announced in Festival Updates and on the festival's social channels. Decisions of the jury are final.

    The Grand Function and winners' announcement takes place on February 12, 2027, at JAIN (Deemed-to-be University), Kochi Campus, Kerala.

You grant the festival a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to screen your film during festival events, use clips and stills for festival promotion, and include your film in festival archives.     Data & Privacy

    Privacy Policy

    How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

    Information We Collect

    When you register a film entry, we collect details you provide such as your name, email, phone number, city, film and crew information, payment reference details, and — for Campus Track entries — a student verification document, which is stored privately and used only to confirm eligibility.

    How We Use Your Information

    Your information is used solely to process your submission, verify entry payments, communicate updates about the festival, and coordinate the judging process.

    Data Sharing

    We do not sell or rent your personal information. Limited details may be shared internally with the jury panel and organizing team strictly for evaluation purposes.

    Data Security

    We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

    Your Rights

    You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by reaching out through our contact helpdesk.

    Refund Policy

    Refunds & Cancellations

    Understand our refund policy and the circumstances under which refunds may apply.

    Non-Refundable Entry Fee

    The entry fee of ₹1,000 is strictly non-refundable once a successful payment has been confirmed by our server and your film entry has been registered. This is standard practice for film festivals worldwide and allows us to cover evaluation, operational, and administrative costs.

    Failed Payment / Technical Error

    If your payment is debited from your bank account or card but your entry registration fails due to a technical error on our payment gateway or server, you are entitled to a full refund of ₹1,000. To claim this refund, contact us within 7 days at sharankrishnashortfilmawards@gmail.com with your name, registered email address, and the Cashfree Order ID. Verified refunds will be processed within 7–10 working days to the original payment method.

    Duplicate Payment

    If you are charged more than once for the same entry due to a technical error, the duplicate charge will be refunded in full within 7–10 working days upon verification. Contact us with both Order IDs.

    Withdrawal by Participant

    If a Participant chooses to withdraw their film entry after successful payment and registration, no refund will be issued. The entry fee is forfeited upon registration.

    Festival Cancellation or Postponement

    In the unlikely event that the Festival is cancelled or significantly postponed due to circumstances beyond our control (force majeure, natural disaster, government restrictions, or similar), the organisers will communicate the situation and available options — which may include entry carryover to a rescheduled event — on a case-by-case basis. Refunds in such cases are at the organisers' discretion.

    Disqualification

    Entries disqualified for violation of submission guidelines or content standards are not eligible for a refund of the entry fee.

    How to Request a Refund

    Email sharankrishnashortfilmawards@gmail.com with Subject: "Refund Request — [Your Name]". Include your registered email, Cashfree Order ID, and reason. We will respond within 3 working days.
