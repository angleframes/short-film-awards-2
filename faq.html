Frequently Asked Questions — Sharankrishna Short Film Awards

Last updated: September 2026

What is Sharankrishna Short Film Awards?

Sharankrishna Short Film Awards is a short film competition honoring the legacy of Sharankrishna, open to filmmakers of all nationalities. The festival runs a General Track open to all filmmakers and a Campus Track for college students, hosted by Angle Frames in association with JAIN (Deemed-to-be University), Kochi. Read the story behind the awards.

Who is eligible to submit a short film?

The General Track is open to filmmakers of all nationalities. Participants must be at least 18 years of age, or have the consent of a parent or guardian if under 18. The Campus Track is for students and requires proof of active institutional enrollment (a Student ID card scan). Full requirements are listed in the Rules & Guidelines.

How do I submit a short film to Sharankrishna Short Film Awards?

Entries are submitted online through the registration form on this website. You enter your details, your film's title, category, runtime and a working YouTube or Drive link, and your cast & crew credits, then review your entry and pay the entry fee securely online. A step-by-step walkthrough is on the How To Submit page, or you can start your submission now.

What is the submission deadline for 2026?

Entries close on December 31, 2026. Filmmakers are encouraged to complete the process on the How To Submit page well ahead of the deadline.

What is the entry fee?

The entry fee is ₹1,000 per submission, paid securely online via Cashfree (UPI, cards, net banking or wallets). The fee is non-refundable once payment is confirmed — see Refunds & Cancellations for details.

What is the maximum runtime for a short film?

Films must not exceed 30 minutes, including opening and closing credits. Films must also have been completed on or after January 1, 2024. See the full Rules & Guidelines.

What is the difference between the General Track and Campus Track?

The General Track is open to any filmmaker. The Campus Track is reserved for currently enrolled students and requires proof of active enrollment. See the Rules & Guidelines for track-specific requirements.

What do winners receive?

Winners across the award categories are honoured at the Grand Function and winners' announcement on February 12, 2027. See all award categories.

Can I submit more than one short film?

Yes. Filmmakers can submit multiple short films. Each film must be submitted as a separate entry, and the ₹1,000 entry fee must be paid separately for each submission. See How To Submit for the submission steps.

Does my film need English subtitles?

Yes, if your film is in any language other than Malayalam. Films in any language other than Malayalam must include English subtitles. This allows the jury to understand and evaluate the film properly. Malayalam-language films do not require English subtitles. See the Rules & Guidelines for the other submission requirements.

Who judges the submissions?

Entries are evaluated by the festival's official jury panel. Jury members will be announced in Festival Updates and on the festival's social channels. Decisions of the jury are final.

When and where is the awards ceremony?

The Grand Function and winners' announcement takes place on February 12, 2027, at JAIN (Deemed-to-be University), Kochi Campus, Kerala.

Who owns the rights to submitted films?

Filmmakers keep ownership of their work. By submitting, you confirm that you own, or have all necessary rights and licences for, all content in the film, including footage, music and any third-party material. You grant the festival a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to screen your film during festival events, use clips and stills for festival promotion, and include your film in festival archives. Read the full Terms & Conditions.

How do I stay updated on announcements and deadline changes?

Follow Festival Updates or the festival's official channels on Instagram, YouTube and Facebook for jury reveals, shortlist announcements and any deadline changes.
